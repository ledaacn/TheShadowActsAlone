# TheShadowActsAlone
The Final Project
## Short Description
An interactive installation where your digital shadow gradually stops obeying you and begins to act on its own.
## Concept
This project explores the unstable relationship between the physical body and its digital reflection.
Most body-based interactive installations are built around a familiar logic: the participant moves, and the digital image responds. The screen becomes a kind of obedient mirror. Instead of making that mirror visually more complex, this project questions the relationship itself.
At first, the digital shadow behaves exactly as expected, following the participant in real time and establishing a clear sense of recognition and control. As the interaction continues, however, this relationship begins to break down. The shadow may continue moving after the participant stops, perform actions the participant never made, or separate into another version of itself. What initially appears to be a reflection gradually begins to feel like an independent digital double.
The work uses an intentionally minimal visual language — a black background and a white human silhouette — so that attention is focused not on visual spectacle, but on behaviour. The key moment is the instant when the participant recognises the figure as themselves, yet also realises that they are no longer fully controlling it.
Rather than presenting complete loss of control, the project creates a small tension between obedience and autonomy, asking a simple question:
**What happens when your reflection begins to have a mind of its own?**
